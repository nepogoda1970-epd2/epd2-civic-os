## Foundation only — **not** fully implemented

Each entry below gets a governed shape it can be built on. None of the
domains is implemented, and none of these entries may be marked
`implemented`.

| FIR                                                               | What PACK-11 provides                                                                                                                                                                                                          | What remains                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `FIR-DEC-001` Governed minutes                                    | `DocumentKind.MEETING_MINUTES`, `OFFICIAL_RECORD_KINDS` (mandatory substantive review), immutable versions, approval, publication                                                                                              | The minutes _content model_ — leadership, secretary, attendance, quorum, agenda version, motions, amendments, results, decisions, objections, role changes, start and end. | `FIR-DEC-002` Separate Decision Register                          | `DocumentKind.DECISION_RECORD`, supersession history, stable ids, versioning                                                                                                                                                   | The register itself: outcome, vote result, responsible body, due date, execution status, completion evidence. | `FIR-CAND-001` Candidacy & Nomination                             | `DocumentKind.CANDIDACY_DOCUMENT`, `NOMINATION_PACKAGE`; versioned candidate documents; the appeal-record kind                                                                                                                 | Eligibility, deadlines, admission, rejection, appeal, election-process linkage, conflict checks. | `FIR-COMM-001` Communications & Official Correspondence           | `DocumentKind.OFFICIAL_CORRESPONDENCE`, `OFFICIAL_NOTICE_PROOF`; retention and legal-hold bindings; governed attachments                                                                                                       | Messages, delivery and read status, moderation, channels, governed templates. PACK-11 records PACK-09's answer and refuses without it — and deliberately does not cache it across acts.                                                                                                                                                                  |

## New FIR entries created by implementation discovery

**None.** Every gap this round encountered was already named in the
register or in ADR-053, and the two limitations it _found_ —
`FIR-INV-010` being satisfiable only as tamper evidence without an external
anchor, and PACK-09's hold state being read-through rather than cached —
are recorded in `docs/handover/PACK-11-KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md` and in the
register's PACK-11 status notes rather than as new entries, because neither
is a new requirement: both are the known boundary of an existing one.
