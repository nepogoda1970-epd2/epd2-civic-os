# FRONT-01 Public Information Architecture

The top-level navigation is intentionally limited to:

1. Über uns
2. Ziele
3. Programm
4. Mitmachen
5. Organisation
6. Transparenz
7. Technologie
8. Status

Legal and accessibility pages remain in the footer. Future capabilities appear
as explanatory pages linked from “So funktioniert es”, not as active services.

The normative WS-01 catalog uses `/home`, `/principles`, `/participate`,
`/structure`, `/news` and `/status`. FRONT-01 retains the established German
public URLs as accepted equivalents and documents them in the route catalog.
The homepage is `/`; `/grundsaetze`, `/mitmachen` and `/struktur` are the
German equivalents of the first-wave normative concepts.

All pages have exactly one `h1`, semantic header/main/footer landmarks, a
keyboard skip link, visible focus, responsive navigation and an accessible,
visible, machine-readable maturity banner.
